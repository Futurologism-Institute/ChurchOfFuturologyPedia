Real Numbers

The real numbers ℝ form a complete ordered field: a field with a total order compatible with the field operations, and every non-empty subset that is bounded above has a least upper bound (supremum).
Archimedean Property
∀x,y > 0 ∃n∈ℕ (n x > y)
Dedekind Completeness
Every non-empty bounded-above set has a least upper bound in ℝ.
