Central Dogma of Molecular Biology

Information flow in biological systems is described by the directed relations:
DNA → DNA (replication)
DNA → RNA (transcription)
RNA → Protein (translation)
Original Statement (Crick)
Once information has passed into protein it cannot get out again. Transfer from protein to protein or protein to nucleic acid is prohibited under the classical dogma.
Modern Extensions
Reverse transcription (RNA → DNA) and RNA replication exist in certain systems (retroviruses, RNA viruses) but remain constrained exceptions.
