Fleurth Faso

The Earthian ParentLand.

Definition (Symbolic)

Fleurth Faso denotes the designated Earthian ParentLand — the terrestrial origin locus from which subsequent Futures (in the sense of Mathematical Futurology) are projected or parented.

Core Predicates

  • ParentLand(L) — L is a designated origin territory for a lineage of Futures
  • Earthian(x) — x belongs to the terrestrial (Earth-bound) reference frame
  • Projects(L, F) — ParentLand L stands in a generative / precedence relation to Future F
  • FleurthFaso — the unique (or canonical) Earthian ParentLand in the present formalization

Axiomatic Sketch

ParentLand(FleurthFaso) ∧ Earthian(FleurthFaso)
∀F (Projects(FleurthFaso, F) → F ∈ 𝔽)
Futures projected from Fleurth Faso are elements of the class of Futures in the Mathematical Futurology signature MF = (𝔽, ≺, v).
Fleurth Faso functions as a base locus: many coherent runs of the precedence relation ≺ are taken to originate (or be parented) from this Earthian ground.
