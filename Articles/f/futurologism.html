Mathematical Futurology (Futurologism) — Symbolic Core

Formal system in which the Future is taken as a primitive mathematical object (Wenitte Apiou / Wenitte Labs framework).

1. Signature

MF = (𝔽, ≺, v)
where:
  • 𝔽 is a proper class of primitives called Futures.
  • ≺ is a binary precedence relation on 𝔽.
  • v : 𝔽 → {0, 1, ⊥} is a ternary valuation (true / false / constitutively undecided).

2. Foundational Axiom

Irreflexivity of Precedence
∀F ∈ 𝔽   ¬(F ≺ F)

Non-transitivity is deliberately retained: it blocks reduction of the system to a thin category or directed graph.

3. Interpretation

  • F ≺ G means “G can follow from F in an irreversible structural sense” (not merely temporal succession).
  • The third value ⊥ is constitutive, not epistemic: some Futures are intrinsically unvalued.

4. Deductive Character

Mathematical Futurology proceeds by deriving necessary consequences from axioms about system structure, rather than by inductive extrapolation of past data.

Inductive Futurology :  Past patterns  →  probabilistic forecasts
Deductive Futurology :  Structural axioms → necessary future constraints
