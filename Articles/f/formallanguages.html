DFA
M = (Q, Σ, δ, q₀, F) with δ : Q × Σ → Q total.
L(M) = {w | δ̂(q₀,w) ∈ F}
Pumping Lemma (Regular)
If L is regular then ∃p such that any w ∈ L with |w|≥p can be written xyz with |xy|≤p, |y|≥1, and xyᵏz ∈ L for all k≥0.
