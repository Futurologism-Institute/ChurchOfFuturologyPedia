Point-Set Topology

A topology on a set X is a collection τ of subsets of X such that:
  1. ∅ ∈ τ and X ∈ τ
  2. τ is closed under arbitrary unions
  3. τ is closed under finite intersections
Members of τ are called open sets.
Continuous Function
f : (X,τ) → (Y,σ) is continuous iff ∀V ∈ σ, f⁻¹(V) ∈ τ.
Hausdorff (T₂)
∀ distinct x,y ∃ open U∋x, V∋y with U∩V=∅.
Compactness
Every open cover has a finite subcover.
