Type Theory (Simply Typed λ-Calculus & Beyond)

Simple Types
Types are generated by: base types | σ → τ
Typing Rules (selected)
Γ, x:σ ⊢ x : σ  (variable)
Γ, x:σ ⊢ M : τ  ⇒  Γ ⊢ λx.M : σ→τ  (abstraction)
Γ ⊢ M : σ→τ  and  Γ ⊢ N : σ  ⇒  Γ ⊢ M N : τ  (application)
Strong Normalization
Every well-typed term in the simply typed λ-calculus has a unique normal form; every reduction sequence terminates.
Curry–Howard Correspondence
Propositions ↔ Types
Proofs ↔ Terms
Provability ↔ Inhabitation
