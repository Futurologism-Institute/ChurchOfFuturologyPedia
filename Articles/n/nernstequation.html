Nernst Equation

Equilibrium potential for an ion species across a membrane:
E = (RT)/(zF) ln ([ion]ₒ / [ion]ᵢ)
or at body temperature (≈37 °C) for monovalent ions approximately
E (mV) ≈ 61.5 log₁₀ ([ion]ₒ / [ion]ᵢ)
Physiological Role
Determines the reversal potential for Na⁺, K⁺, Ca²⁺, Cl⁻ and is fundamental to resting membrane potential and action-potential dynamics.
