Norse Mythology (Core Structure)

Cosmology
Nine worlds connected by Yggdrasil (the World Tree). Key realms: Asgard (gods), Midgard (humans), Jötunheim (giants), Hel, Niflheim, Muspelheim.
Principal Deities
Odin (Allfather, wisdom, war), Thor (thunder, protection), Freyja (love, seiðr), Freyr (fertility), Loki (trickster), Tyr (justice), Heimdall (watchman), Frigg (queen).
Eschatology
Ragnarök: the prophesied destruction and renewal of the world, involving the death of major gods and the emergence of a new cycle.
