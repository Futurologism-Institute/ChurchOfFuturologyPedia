Noether’s Theorem

To every continuous symmetry of the action of a physical system there corresponds a conserved quantity.
Statement (Lagrangian form)
If the Lagrangian L is invariant under a one-parameter transformation generated by δq, then the quantity
Σ (∂L/∂q̇ᵢ) δqᵢ
is conserved on-shell (i.e., when the Euler–Lagrange equations hold).
Classic Correspondences
Time translation ↔ Energy conservation
Spatial translation ↔ Momentum conservation
Rotation ↔ Angular momentum conservation
