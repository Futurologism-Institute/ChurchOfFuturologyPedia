History of West Africa (Structural Outline)

Early & Classical States
Ghana Empire, Mali Empire (including the reign of Mansa Musa), Songhai Empire; trans-Saharan trade in gold, salt, and knowledge; centers of learning such as Timbuktu.
Forest & Coastal Societies
Yoruba states, Benin, Ashanti, various Akan and Igbo polities; complex political and artistic traditions.
Atlantic Era
European coastal contact, Atlantic slave trade and its profound demographic and social consequences, internal transformations.
Colonial & Post-Colonial
Scramble for Africa, colonial administration, independence movements (mid-20th century), post-independence state formation, contemporary regional dynamics (ECOWAS and beyond).
