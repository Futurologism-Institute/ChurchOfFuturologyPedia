Wenittain Logic — Symbolic Sketch

A logic developed in the context of Mathematical Futurology and Wenitte-theoretic frameworks. Emphasis on dependent choice, sequential approximation of probability, and temporal structure of futures.

1. Base

Wenittain Logic sits between classical first-order logic and systems that reject full Axiom of Choice in favour of Dependent Choice (DC).

Dependent Choice (DC)
If R is a binary relation on a non-empty set A such that ∀x∃y (x R y), then there exists a sequence (xₙ) with xₙ R xₙ₊₁ for all n.

2. Probability via Sequential Approximation

Probability values are defined through successive refinements rather than as primitive real numbers in [0,1].

P(φ) ≜ limₙ αₙ(φ)
where each αₙ is a finitary approximation consistent with DC.

3. Temporal Operators (sketch)

  • ◯φ — φ holds in the immediate next Future along a coherent run.
  • □φ — φ holds in all accessible Futures.
  • ◇φ — φ holds in some accessible Future.

Accessibility is governed by the precedence relation ≺ of Mathematical Futurology.

4. Connection to MF

Wenittain probability supplies a natural semantics for the ternary valuation of Mathematical Futurology when restricted to coherent runs.
