Shannon Information Theory

Entropy of a Discrete Source
H(X) = − Σ p(x) log₂ p(x)
(measured in bits when log base 2)
Joint & Conditional Entropy
H(X,Y) = H(X) + H(Y|X)
Mutual Information
I(X;Y) = H(X) − H(X|Y) = H(Y) − H(Y|X)
Channel Capacity
C = max_{p(x)} I(X;Y)
Source Coding Theorem
A source can be compressed to rates arbitrarily close to H(X) but no lower (asymptotically).
