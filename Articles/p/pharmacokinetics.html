Pharmacokinetics (One-Compartment Model)

For intravenous bolus in a one-compartment model:
C(t) = C₀ e^{−kt}
where k is the elimination rate constant.
Half-life
t_{1/2} = ln(2)/k ≈ 0.693/k
Clearance & Volume
Cl = k · V_d
AUC = Dose / Cl
ADME
Absorption, Distribution, Metabolism, Excretion — the four fundamental processes governing drug concentration over time.
