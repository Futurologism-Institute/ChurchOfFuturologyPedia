Photosynthesis

Overall Equation
6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O + light → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6 O₂
Light-Dependent Reactions
Occur in thylakoid membranes; produce ATP and NADPH; release O₂ from water.
Calvin Cycle (Light-Independent)
Carbon fixation via Rubisco; reduction and regeneration phases; net production of sugars.
