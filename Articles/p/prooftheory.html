Proof Theory (Natural Deduction Sketch)

Focus on Gentzen-style natural deduction for classical and intuitionistic propositional/first-order logic.

Introduction & Elimination Rules (selected)
∧I: from φ and ψ infer φ∧ψ
∧E: from φ∧ψ infer φ (or ψ)
→I: from [φ] … ψ infer φ→ψ (discharge assumption)
→E (Modus Ponens): from φ→ψ and φ infer ψ
∀I: from φ(x) (x not free in open assumptions) infer ∀x φ(x)
∀E: from ∀x φ(x) infer φ(t)
Normalization
Every derivation can be reduced to a normal form without detours (maximum formulas).
Cut-Elimination (Gentzen)
In sequent calculus, the Cut rule is admissible; every proof has a cut-free version.
