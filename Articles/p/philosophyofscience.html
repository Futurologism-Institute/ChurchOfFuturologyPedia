Philosophy of Science

Popper — Falsifiability
A theory is scientific only if it is falsifiable in principle.
Scientific(T) → ∃ observation O such that O would refute T
Kuhn — Paradigms & Revolutions
Normal science proceeds within a paradigm. Scientific revolutions involve paradigm shifts that are not purely cumulative or fully rational in the classical sense.
Lakatos — Research Programmes
A research programme consists of a hard core + protective belt. Progressive vs degenerating problem-shifts.
Bayesian Confirmation
P(H|E) = P(E|H)P(H)/P(E)
Evidence E confirms H when P(H|E) > P(H).
