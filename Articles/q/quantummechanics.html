Quantum Mechanics — Dirac / von Neumann Postulates

I. State Space
The pure state of an isolated system is represented by a unit vector |ψ⟩ in a complex Hilbert space ℋ.
II. Observables
Physical observables correspond to self-adjoint operators A on ℋ. Possible measurement outcomes are eigenvalues of A.
III. Born Rule
If the system is in state |ψ⟩ and A has spectral decomposition Σ a P_a, the probability of outcome a is ⟨ψ|P_a|ψ⟩.
IV. Measurement (Projection Postulate)
Upon obtaining outcome a the state collapses to P_a|ψ⟩ / ‖P_a|ψ⟩‖ (ideal projective measurement).
V. Time Evolution
Between measurements the state evolves unitarily:
iℏ d|ψ⟩/dt = H|ψ⟩
or equivalently |ψ(t)⟩ = U(t)|ψ(0)⟩ with U(t) = exp(−iHt/ℏ).
VI. Composite Systems
The state space of a composite system is the tensor product of the component Hilbert spaces.
