Quantum Field Theory (Sketch)

Fields are operator-valued distributions on spacetime. Particles arise as excitations of the fields.
Canonical Quantization (scalar field)
Promote φ(x) and π(x) to operators satisfying
[φ(x), π(y)] = i δ³(x−y) (equal-time)
Lagrangian Density (free scalar)
ℒ = ½ ∂_μ φ ∂^μ φ − ½ m² φ²
Path-Integral Formulation
Z[J] = ∫ 𝒟φ exp(i ∫ (ℒ + J φ) d⁴x)
