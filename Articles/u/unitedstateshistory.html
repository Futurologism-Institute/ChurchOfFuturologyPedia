History of the United States (Structural Outline)

Colonial & Revolutionary Period
English, West African, Spanish, French, Dutch colonization; indigenous nations; American Revolution (1775–1783); Constitution (1787) and Bill of Rights.
Expansion & Civil War
Westward expansion, slavery, Civil War (1861–1865), Reconstruction, Industrial Revolution.
20th Century
Progressive Era, World Wars, Great Depression, New Deal, Cold War, Civil Rights Movement, Vietnam, rise of the United States as superpower.
Late 20th – Early 21st Century
End of Cold War, globalization, digital revolution, post-9/11 era, polarization, technological acceleration.
