Enzyme Kinetics (Michaelis–Menten)

The basic catalytic scheme:
E + S ⇌ ES → E + P
Michaelis–Menten Equation
v = V_max [S] / (K_m + [S])
where V_max is the maximum velocity and K_m is the Michaelis constant (substrate concentration at half-maximal velocity).
Lineweaver–Burk
1/v = (K_m / V_max)(1/[S]) + 1/V_max
