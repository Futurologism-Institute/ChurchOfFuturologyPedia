Epistemology — Classical Analysis of Knowledge

Traditional (JTB) Account
S knows that p iff
  1. p is true
  2. S believes that p
  3. S is justified in believing that p
K(S,p) ⇔ T(p) ∧ B(S,p) ∧ J(S,p)
Gettier Problem
There exist cases where JTB holds but intuition denies knowledge (Gettier 1963). Hence JTB is insufficient.
Possible Strengthening
No false lemmas / causal / reliability / tracking (Nozick) / safety conditions, etc.
