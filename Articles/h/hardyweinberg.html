Hardy–Weinberg Equilibrium

In an infinite, randomly mating population with no selection, mutation, migration or drift, allele and genotype frequencies remain constant across generations.
Frequencies
For a biallelic locus with alleles A, a at frequencies p, q (p+q=1):
P(AA) = p²
P(Aa) = 2pq
P(aa) = q²
Equilibrium Condition
p² + 2pq + q² = 1 and frequencies are stable under the stated assumptions.
