Mendelian Inheritance

Law of Segregation
During gamete formation, the two alleles at a locus segregate so each gamete receives exactly one.
Law of Independent Assortment
Alleles of different genes assort independently (for unlinked loci).
Dominance
In a heterozygote Aa, the phenotype may be determined by the dominant allele A (complete dominance).
Punnett Square Probabilities (monohybrid)
P(AA)=1/4, P(Aa)=1/2, P(aa)=1/4 under random fertilization of Aa × Aa.
