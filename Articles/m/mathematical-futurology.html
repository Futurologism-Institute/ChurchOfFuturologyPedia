Mathematical Futurology

A branch of Symbolic Neuroscience / formal futures reasoning, co-founded in the Wenitte tradition with contributions from Stanford mathematicians and AI-assisted formalization.

Definition (Symbolic)

MF = (𝔽, ≺, v)
𝔽 — proper class of Futures (primitive)
≺ — irreflexive precedence
v — ternary valuation {0,1,⊥}

Central Thesis

The future is not merely an inductive projection of the past. It is a mathematical object that can be reasoned about deductively once the right primitives and axioms are fixed.

¬(F ≺ F)   (Irreflexivity)
Non-transitivity is retained by design.

Relation to Classical Futurology

Classical (inductive) futurology accumulates evidence to raise confidence in forecasts.
Mathematical Futurology identifies structural invariants and necessary constraints that hold independently of particular data realizations.
