Maxwell’s Equations

Differential Form (SI)
∇ · E = ρ / ε₀   (Gauss’s law)
∇ · B = 0   (no magnetic monopoles)
∇ × E = −∂B/∂t   (Faraday)
∇ × B = μ₀ J + μ₀ ε₀ ∂E/∂t   (Ampère–Maxwell)
Continuity Equation
∇ · J + ∂ρ/∂t = 0 (charge conservation)
Wave Equation (in vacuum)
∇² E − (1/c²) ∂²E/∂t² = 0,   c = 1/√(μ₀ε₀)
