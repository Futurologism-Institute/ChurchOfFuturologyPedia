Greek Mythology (Core Structure)

Cosmogony
Chaos → Gaia (Earth), Tartarus, Eros → Uranus (Sky) → Titans → Olympians.
Olympian Pantheon (selected)
Zeus (sky, king), Hera (marriage), Poseidon (sea), Demeter (harvest), Athena (wisdom), Apollo (light, music), Artemis (hunt), Ares (war), Aphrodite (love), Hephaestus (forge), Hermes (messenger), Dionysus (wine), Hestia (hearth).
Heroic Cycle
Heroes (Heracles, Perseus, Theseus, Odysseus, Achilles) mediate between divine and human realms through labors, quests, and tragic flaws.
