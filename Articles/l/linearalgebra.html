Linear Algebra (Vector Spaces)

A vector space over a field F is a set V with addition and scalar multiplication satisfying the usual 8 axioms (associativity, commutativity, identity, inverses, distributivity, etc.).
Basis & Dimension
A basis is a linearly independent spanning set. All bases have the same cardinality (dimension).
Linear Map
T : V → W is linear iff T(u+v)=T(u)+T(v) and T(αu)=αT(u).
Rank–Nullity
dim V = rank(T) + nullity(T)
Inner Product Space
Additional positive-definite symmetric bilinear form ⟨·,·⟩ yielding norms and orthogonality.
