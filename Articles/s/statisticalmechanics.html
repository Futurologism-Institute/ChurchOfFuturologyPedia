Statistical Mechanics

Microcanonical Ensemble
For an isolated system with energy in [E, E+δE], the entropy is
S = k_B ln Ω
where Ω is the number of accessible microstates.
Canonical Ensemble
Probability of a microstate with energy E_i:
P_i = (1/Z) e^{-β E_i},   β = 1/(k_B T)
Partition function Z = Σ_i e^{-β E_i}
Helmholtz Free Energy
F = −k_B T ln Z = U − T S
Grand Canonical Ensemble
Ξ = Σ e^{-β(E_i − μ N_i)}
