SIR Compartmental Epidemic Model

Population partitioned into Susceptible (S), Infectious (I), Recovered (R) with S+I+R = N (constant).
Differential Equations
dS/dt = −β S I / N
dI/dt = β S I / N − γ I
dR/dt = γ I
where β is the transmission rate and γ the recovery rate.
Basic Reproduction Number
R₀ = β / γ
Epidemic spreads if R₀ > 1.
