History of Silicon Valley

Foundational Period (1930s–1950s)
Stanford University (Frederick Terman) encourages faculty and students to commercialize research. Hewlett-Packard founded 1939. Shockley Semiconductor (1956) and the “Traitorous Eight” found Fairchild Semiconductor (1957).
Integrated Circuit & Microprocessor Era (1960s–1970s)
Fairchild → Intel (1968, Noyce & Moore). Moore’s Law articulated. Apple founded 1976. Venture capital ecosystem crystallizes along Sand Hill Road.
Personal Computing & Internet (1980s–2000s)
Rise of software (Microsoft influence, though not geographically local), Netscape, Google (1998), the dot-com boom and bust, followed by Web 2.0 and social platforms.
Mobile, Cloud & AI Era (2007–present)
iPhone (2007) catalyzes mobile. Cloud computing (AWS et al.), then large-scale machine learning and generative AI become central economic and cultural forces.
