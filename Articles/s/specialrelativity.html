Special Relativity

Postulate 1 (Relativity Principle)
The laws of physics are the same in all inertial frames.
Postulate 2 (Constancy of Light Speed)
The speed of light in vacuum c is the same for all observers, independent of the motion of the source.

Consequences (Symbolic)

  • Lorentz transformations between frames moving at relative velocity v along x:
    x' = γ(x − vt),  t' = γ(t − vx/c²)
    where γ = 1/√(1−v²/c²)
  • Time dilation: Δt = γ Δτ
  • Length contraction: L = L₀ / γ
  • Mass-energy equivalence: E = γ m c²  (rest energy E₀ = m c²)
  • Interval invariance: ds² = c²dt² − dx² − dy² − dz² is invariant.
